~10s. Mercado Global is B2B cross-border: MXN to USD and back through the vault's exchange, priced off the oracle with a size-dependent impact and a fee. On the v2 journey, 200 xMXN was redeemed for 10.86 OUSD at USD/MXN 18.37 and burned, with exchange coverage at 307%; redemptions are bounded by what the exchange issued and by a rolling daily bucket. Partners get a signed indicative quote (30-second expiry, minimum received) through an API they pay for over HTTP 402: the payment buys the quote response, not the supplier payment and not a guaranteed fill. Never call it a firm quote. The screenshot is the live app on v2, showing the exchange reserve and about 300% coverage of the xMXN it issued. The FX rate is real (Coinbase, about 18.37) but admin-relayed on-chain on testnet, not a decentralized oracle.
