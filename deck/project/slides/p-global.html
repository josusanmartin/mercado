~10s. Mercado Global is cross-border for businesses: pay a Mexican supplier in pesos from dollars, or bring pesos back. A real quote right now: US$10,000 pays a supplier MX$176,294.94. At that size, price impact is part of the cost, and the quote shows it; one swap is capped at US$10,000. Partners get a signed indicative quote valid for 30 seconds with the minimum received, through an API they pay for per call. Never call it a firm quote.
