The whole demo journey, J0 to J7, ran green live on Moderato in one run against the final deploy: deposit, mint, swap 50 OUSD for 995 xMXN and send it, borrow 500 against a signed attestation (four negative simulations revert), pay 100 xMXN through a real 402 in both server- and wallet-broadcast modes with an idempotent retry, repay 510 with the pool's realized fees going from 0 to 10, and an FX stress liquidation with the oracle and desk fully restored. Our reviewer independently rechecked every receipt from that run: a replayed borrower nonce reverts with NonceAlreadyUsed, and each bill order maps to exactly one quoted xMXN transfer. 67 of 67 contract tests pass, and all five contracts (oracle, factory, vault, swap desk, credit pool) are source-verified on Tempo's Sourcify verifier with an exact match. On the SPEC acceptance checklist, 9 of 11 checks are signed off, from live runs plus Foundry tests: explorer visibility, vault-only minting and ratio limits, safe withdrawals, oracle price rails, FX stress liquidation, swap limits, credit-pool rejections, lender withdrawal limits and the MPP 402. The two left are the frontend labels and the README disclosure check, waiting on the UI. Every chip links to its contract on the explorer. The orange chip is Tempo fee sponsorship: our relay paid the fees for a brand-new account holding zero fee tokens, limited to Mercado contracts and rate-capped.
