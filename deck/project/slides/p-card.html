08

~8s. Mercado Card is the fifth product: spend xMXN at a shop. The design: a holder issues a virtual card with an on-chain spend limit and daily window and can freeze it; an allowlisted demo merchant terminal charges it, the holder approves with a tap signature, xMXN moves on-chain from holder to merchant, and refunds are real too. Only the card network and the card number are simulated: it's a demo virtual card, not Visa or Mastercard, and it works only at Mercado demo merchants, with no physical card and no ATM. Status: the contract and the app are being built now; don't claim a live charge until it's deployed and its journey passes.
