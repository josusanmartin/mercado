08

~8s. Mercado Card is the fifth product: spend xMXN at a shop. The design: a holder issues a virtual card with an on-chain daily limit that resets at midnight CDMX and can freeze it; an allowlisted demo merchant terminal charges it, the holder approves with a tap signature, xMXN moves on-chain from holder to merchant, and refunds are real too. Only the card network and the card number are simulated: it's a demo virtual card, not Visa or Mastercard, and it works only at Mercado demo merchants, with no physical card and no ATM. Live on v2 testnet: at the demo shop 'Tienda Doña Mary', the holder signed a tap for 25 xMXN, the terminal charged it on-chain, and the shop refunded it on-chain; the app's activity list reads the CardCharged and CardRefunded events. The holder's tap signature is mandatory, so an approved merchant alone can't pull funds; the daily limit resets at midnight CDMX. The adversarial review of the charge authorization is still running, so don't call it audited.
