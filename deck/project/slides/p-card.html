Mercado · testnet · no real value · Tempo Moderato

09

~8s. Mercado Card is the fifth product: spend xMXN at a shop. The design: a holder issues a virtual card with an on-chain daily limit that resets at midnight CDMX and can freeze it; an allowlisted demo merchant terminal charges it, the holder approves with a tap signature, xMXN moves on-chain from holder to merchant, and refunds are real too. Only the card network and the card number are simulated: it's a demo virtual card, not Visa or Mastercard, and it works only at Mercado demo merchants, with no physical card and no ATM. The video (about 40 seconds, all real Tempo transactions) is one person, one peso: Javier connects his demo Uber data, draws a 375 xMXN advance (owing 382.50 with the 2% fee), freezes his Mercado Card, gets declined at 'Tienda Doña Mary' with nothing charged, unfreezes, and the retry pays 150 xMXN exactly once, with receipts on the shop terminal and the phone. Earlier, a 25 xMXN charge was also refunded on-chain; the app's activity list reads the CardCharged and CardRefunded events. The holder's tap signature is mandatory, so an approved merchant alone can't pull funds; the daily limit resets at midnight CDMX. The adversarial review of the charge authorization is still running, so don't call it audited.
