■ 02 · Mercado

Create. Send. Pay. Borrow. Spend. Save.

Move money between products without leaving Mercado.

Six connected products.

Mercado

02

Mint creates pesos; Remesas and Global move them; Crédito lends them; Card spends them; Invest offers savings and an equity-index token. These products share a currency and settlement network, while collateral, exchange reserves, loan capital, and savings remain separate accounts.
