~12s. One currency, six products. Everything runs on xMXN, a synthetic peso on Tempo: a peso economy built from Tempo primitives. The screenshot is the live home page. 01 Mint: institutions create xMXN against collateral. 02 Remesas: send money home. 03 Global: pay suppliers across the border. 04 Crédito: advances for gig workers, funded by lenders. 05 Card: spend xMXN at a shop. 06 Invest: save in pesos, dollars or demo T-bills. How money moves between them is on the 'why it compounds' slide. Testnet prototype: OUSD has no real value, demo tokens have admin-set yield and prices, xMXN has no peg or fiat redemption.
