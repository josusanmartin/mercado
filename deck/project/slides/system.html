02

~15s. Mercado is a full ecosystem: six products around one synthetic peso, xMXN. Mercado Mint (jade): institutions lock collateral (OUSD, demo staked OUSD, demo tokenized T-bills) and mint xMXN; repaying burns it. Mercado Remesas (marigold): a sender's OUSD is minted straight into xMXN for family in Mexico through the vault's exchange. Mercado Global (cobalt): businesses redeem xMXN back to OUSD, which burns it, using signed indicative quotes. Mercado Crédito (rosa): lenders fund a pool, drivers borrow and repay into the pool, so those repayments return to lenders rather than burning supply. Mercado Card (violet) is where xMXN gets spent at a shop; it is being built now, with a demo card network. Mercado Invest (gold) is savings: pesos into the lender pool, dollars into staked OUSD, and demo T-bills that can then back Mint, which closes the loop. And every product pays for bills and APIs through the same HTTP 402 rail on Tempo. All of this ran green on Moderato testnet; testnet OUSD has no real value, the demo tokens' yield and prices are admin-set, and xMXN has no peg or fiat redemption.
