02

~12s. One currency, six products. Everything runs on xMXN, a synthetic peso on Tempo. Mercado Mint: institutions create xMXN against collateral. Remesas: send money home. Global: pay suppliers across the border. Crédito: advances for gig workers, funded by lenders. Card: spend xMXN at a shop. Invest: save in pesos, dollars or demo T-bills. How money moves between them is on the 'why it compounds' slide. Testnet prototype: OUSD has no real value, demo tokens have admin-set yield and prices, xMXN has no peg or fiat redemption.
