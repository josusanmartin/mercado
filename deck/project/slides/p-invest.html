~8s. Mercado Invest is the savings product, with three ways to grow money. Peso savings: deposit xMXN into the real Crédito lending pool; the return is the driver fees actually realized, so far 20 xMXN in pool v2, historical and variable, never guaranteed, and only idle cash is withdrawable. Dollar savings: stake OUSD into sOUSD, the real ERC-4626 on v2, whose accrual comes from a demo yield reserve the admin funds on testnet. T-bills: buy and sell the demo tokenized T-bill through a new InvestDesk at the admin feed price plus a spread, then use it as Mint collateral; all three are live on v2 and were verified in the browser: 10 xMXN supplied to the pool, 10 OUSD into sOUSD (demo APR 5%), and 10 OUSD buying 0.09975 tTBILL on the desk at the $100 admin price plus spread. The tTBILL is a demo, not a security; its issuance is gated (no faucet), and the desk only buys back what it sold to you. Never say guaranteed returns.
