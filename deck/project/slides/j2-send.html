~10s. Move is remittances and cross-border payments. In v2 the vault is also the exchange: OUSD coming in from a US sender mints xMXN directly, and xMXN going out is burned to redeem OUSD, priced off the oracle with a virtual-reserve curve for size-dependent rates. Its solvency limits (OUSD reserve against xMXN outstanding when the peso strengthens) are enforced on-chain. v2 design (crew/V2-DESIGN.md; deployed on Moderato and independently checked, journey pending, not yet the app default): MoveAMM is a separate contract with its own OUSD reserve that never touches CDP collateral, priced off the oracle mid with a size-dependent impact plus a fee. A stronger peso reduces how much USD backs the xMXN it issued; the reserve coverage, redemption-stop or haircut policy and recapitalization path are still being specified and reviewed, so don't promise immediate redemption or describe those rules as final. The vAMM is deployed alongside v1 but not yet the active path; only call it mint/burn execution once there's a v2 journey receipt. Already live: GET /api/move/quote returns the rate, oracle mid, spread, fee and size impact (reading the v1 swap desk until the vAMM deploys), and partners such as a remittance app can buy a signed quote over MPP for 0.50 xMXN, so Move and Spend share the same 402 rail. The partner pays for the quote response, not for the swap itself or a guaranteed fill after the quote expires. Live: a partner bot paid 0.50 xMXN over 402 and got a signed quote for 250 OUSD → 4,975 xMXN (50 bps from the mock mid, from the v1 swap desk); the signature verifies against Mercado's quote key (the "paid quote" link). What's live today is the separately funded swap desk: 50 testnet OUSD in, 995 xMXN out at the mock 20 MXN/USD minus a 50 bps spread, sent to the family wallet (the link is that live swap). On-chain delivery only; there's no SPEI or bank cash-out in this prototype.
