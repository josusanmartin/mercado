~10s. Mercado Remesas is mobile-first, for a sender in the US paying family in Mexico. On the v2 journey, 50 testnet OUSD went into the vault's exchange and 997.8 xMXN was minted straight to the recipient in one transaction, with slippage and size limits enforced. The same app pays Tiempo Aire and services for the family through HTTP 402; the payment is real testnet xMXN, the Telcel top-up is SIMULATED. Delivery is on-chain only: there's no SPEI or bank cash-out in this prototype. The app shows it inside an Android phone.
