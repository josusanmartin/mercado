~8s, keep it brief. A sender swaps 50 testnet OUSD on the swap desk: the mock 20 MXN/USD minus a 50 bps spread is 995 xMXN, exactly what the live run delivered, with a minOut slippage guard. The desk pays from its own seeded, finite reserves and can't touch vault collateral; it's a capped demo liquidity source, not a market. The xMXN goes to the recipient's wallet. There's no SPEI or bank cash-out in this prototype. Live on Moderato: swap 50 OUSD for 995 xMXN, then transfer 995 xMXN to the recipient.
