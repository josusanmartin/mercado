~10s. Mercado Remesas is mobile-first, for a sender in the US paying family in Mexico. On the v2 journey, 50 testnet OUSD went into the vault's exchange and 916.69 xMXN was minted straight to the recipient in one transaction: the oracle mid of USD/MXN 18.37 (admin-relayed from Coinbase) less a 20 bps fee (1.84 xMXN) and 2 bps of size impact, with slippage and size limits enforced. The same app pays Tiempo Aire and services for the family through HTTP 402; the payment is real testnet xMXN, the Telcel top-up is SIMULATED. Delivery is on-chain only: there's no SPEI or bank cash-out in this prototype. The app shows it inside an Android phone. The screenshot is the live app on v2: the send runs through the Mercado exchange, which mints xMXN to the family. A card top-up ('Agregar dólares con tarjeta': a simulated checkout that delivers real OUSD on-chain) is built but switched off until its security review passes, so don't demo or claim it; the app shows 'Próximamente'.
