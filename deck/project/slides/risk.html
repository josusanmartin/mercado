A3

Two separate balance sheets fail differently. In Mint, each position is liquidated at its own collateral's threshold; the 280% aggregate backing is a reference, not a liquidation trigger. The exchange redeems only from its own OUSD reserve, at the lower of oracle value and NAV, through a leaky-bucket rate limit, and stops minting below 100% coverage. Owner-controlled prices and settings remain part of the trust model.
