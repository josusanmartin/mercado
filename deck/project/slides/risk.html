~8s. Every xMXN is a peso debt backed by dollar-valued collateral, so a stronger peso is the core risk. On a 250% position, starting from the real rate, USD/MXN 18.37 admin-relayed from Coinbase, a 250% position (1,000 OUSD, 7,348 xMXN) crosses the 135% liquidation line near 9.92 MXN/USD, the dollar falling 46% against the peso; demo tokens have their own ratios (the T-bill liquidates below 170%). The animation is a preview only; the link is a real v1 liquidation on Moderato (the v2 stress step is still pending) of a dedicated 160% position, made earlier with the old mock oracle force-set from 20 to 13 and then restored. The exchange has its own reserve, bounded redemptions and an equity buffer; those limits are enforced on-chain, and they reduce, not remove, the risk. Testnet OUSD has no real value.
