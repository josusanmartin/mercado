■ A8 · Risk

Check the backing before you borrow.

A stronger peso leaves less room to borrow safely.

Mercado

A8

Peso debt gets harder to cover when the peso strengthens against dollar-valued collateral, so the app shows the effect on backing and individual positions. Positions can be liquidated and exchange redemptions can be restricted or take a haircut; owner-controlled prices and settings remain part of the trust model.
