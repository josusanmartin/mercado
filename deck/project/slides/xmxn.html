A10

Appendix for Q&A. There are two ways xMXN comes into existence. Borrowing: lock collateral in the MultiCollateralVault and mint up to its value divided by the mint ratio; one thousand OUSD at 18.37 and 150% gives 12,246.67 xMXN of debt, liquidated below 135%. Buying: send OUSD to MoveAMM and receive the oracle mid less a 0.20% fee and a size impact (D is a pricing parameter, not funded liquidity); one thousand OUSD quoted 18,256 xMXN at 10:21 UTC. The vault's backing is collateral against debt; the AMM's is its own OUSD reserve against the xMXN it issued, and it can only redeem up to that liability. The FX rate is admin-relayed from Coinbase, and xMXN is not redeemable for bank pesos.
