~10s. Mercado Mint is for institutions and sophisticated holders: lock collateral (dollars, staked dollars, and tokenized assets) and mint pesos, xMXN, while the collateral keeps its own yield. The math is the product's own: US$4M of collateral at today's 18.37 is worth MX$73.48M; at the 150% minimum ratio that mints up to MX$48.99M, or MX$36.74M at the recommended 200%. Each collateral has its own mint line, liquidation line and debt ceiling. Q&A context: running on Tempo's testnet today; collateral assets there are test assets.
