04

~10s. Create is institutional money creation. Institutions lock collateral and mint xMXN; v2 accepts yield-bearing collateral (staked OUSD, tokenized T-bills) whose yield accrues inside the collateral token while it's locked, so idle collateral can create money that enters circulation. On testnet, staked OUSD and T-bills are demo tokens with admin-set yield and prices, being built as v2 alongside the live v1; no real T-bill backing or real yield is claimed. What's live today is the OUSD vault: 1,000 testnet OUSD (faucet, no real value) mints 8,000 xMXN at the mock 20 MXN/USD, 250%; the vault refuses anything under 150%. v2 design (crew/V2-DESIGN.md, approved design, not yet deployed): per-collateral positions with their own ratios and debt ceilings. OUSD and demo sOUSD mint at 150% and liquidate below 135%; the demo tokenized T-bill (tTBILL) gets a bigger haircut, minting at 200% and liquidating below 170%. sOUSD's mock yield is accrual-based and capped, so donations can't pump its share price. The minter keeps the yield: their ratio rises and they can withdraw the excess. The animation is the risk we show: a stronger peso lowers the ratio, which crosses the 135% liquidation line near 10.8 MXN/USD. The link is a real on-chain liquidation of a dedicated 160% position, with the mock oracle restored afterwards.
