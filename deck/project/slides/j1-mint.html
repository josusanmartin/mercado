~12s. Supporting slide: where the driver's pesos come from. The LP locks 1,000 testnet OUSD (Tempo faucet, no real value) and mints 8,000 xMXN at the mock 20 MXN/USD: 250%. The vault refuses anything under 150%. A USD-backed peso debt gets riskier as the peso strengthens: the ratio crosses the 135% liquidation line at about 10.8 MXN/USD, the dollar falling 46% against the peso. In the app this is a preview that never writes the shared oracle. The link is a real liquidation on Moderato from the full journey run: a dedicated 160% position, oracle force-set 20 to 13, liquidated, then the price restored and the desk unpaused.
