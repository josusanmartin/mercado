04

~10s. Mercado Mint is for institutions and sophisticated holders: lock collateral, mint xMXN, keep the collateral's own yield. On the v2 journey on Moderato, at the real rate (USD/MXN 18.37, admin-relayed from Coinbase), 200 OUSD was staked into demo sOUSD and 1,469.93 xMXN minted against it at 250%; a separate position deposited 10 demo tokenized T-bills (priced at $100 each, about 18,374 xMXN of value) and minted 6,124.71 xMXN at 300%. T-bills need at least 200% to mint and liquidate below 170%. Over-mint and over-withdraw simulations revert, and only the vault and exchange can issue. sOUSD and tTBILL are demo tokens with admin-set yield and prices: no real T-bill backing, no real yield. Repaying burns the xMXN.
