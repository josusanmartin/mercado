Appendix for Q&A. We start with one corridor and one income source: a licensed MXN payout partner so a US remittance can be cashed out in pesos, and one approved driver-platform earnings integration. We measure completed cash-outs, repeat spend and repayment. Everything else is for questions only: issuer-backed xMXN with attestations and redemption, a contracted airtime and bill aggregator, a verified USD/MXN feed, and SPEI cash-out. None of this touches real customers or money until Mexican legal and compliance specialists have reviewed it.
