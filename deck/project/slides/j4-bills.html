~20s. Spend: HTTP 402 payments settle in xMXN. Slow down here. The driver spends 100 MXN of the advance on Telcel airtime. The unpaid request gets a real HTTP 402 from mppx; the wallet pays in xMXN on Tempo; the server verifies that exact payment for that exact order before calling the provider. Under the log, the payment sits next to the remaining debt: 100 xMXN paid, 306 still owed. The video on the right is the real 48-second recording of the whole Lend-to-Spend flow as Javier (score 96.5, draw 300, Telcel 100 over 402, receipt); its poster frame is the receipt. It plays on its own when presented, and it's the backup if the live demo misbehaves. The top-up is simulated: MPP is the payment rail, not a bill-payment provider, and no airtime is delivered. A settled order returns its recorded receipt on retry, and a crash-and-restart test recovered a paid order from the chain without charging twice. The live link is that recorded payment on Moderato.
