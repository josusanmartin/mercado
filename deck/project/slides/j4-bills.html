~25s. Slow down here. The driver spends 100 MXN of the advance on Telcel airtime. The unpaid request gets a real HTTP 402 from mppx; the wallet pays in xMXN on Tempo; the server verifies that exact payment for that exact order before calling the provider. The bottom row puts the receipt next to the remaining debt: 400 xMXN left, 510 still owed. The top-up is simulated: MPP is the payment rail, not a bill-payment provider, and no airtime is delivered. A settled order returns its recorded receipt on retry; recovery from an interrupted payment is still under test, so don't claim more. The live link is the full-journey run on Moderato: the driver paid 100 xMXN to the merchant through a real 402, with an idempotent retry. Both server-broadcast (pull) and wallet-broadcast (push, what Tempo Wallet and passkeys use) modes pass live. The driver later repays 510 by hand from separate, labeled test funds.
