03

~10s. Five problems, one per job, in plain words and with no statistics; don't add numbers we haven't sourced. Institutions hold dollar collateral that earns but does not create local money. Families sending money home go through FX spreads and intermediaries. Gig drivers earn weekly and unevenly, and credit isn't shaped for that. And everyday spending is priced in pesos. Mercado puts all four on one synthetic peso, on Tempo.
