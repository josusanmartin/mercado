~12s. One person carries the whole pitch: a gig driver who needs 100 MXN of airtime before payday. The bars are the "irregular" Demo Uber Data profile, which is synthetic: weekly net pay in MXN, and week 4 earned nothing. This is our demo scenario, not market data, so don't add statistics. Everything that follows is one xMXN flow: a lender funds a pool, the driver draws a small advance, and pays for the top-up over HTTP 402.
