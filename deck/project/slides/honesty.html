~8s. Say this plainly. Token issuance, vault accounting, swaps, loans and the 402 payment are real testnet transactions. The collateral is Tempo's faucet OUSD: no real value, not issuer-backed, not redeemable. Staked OUSD and tokenized T-bills are demo tokens with admin-set yield and prices: no real T-bill backing and no real yield. The FX oracle is an admin mock, the Uber data is synthetic, the airtime top-up is simulated, and repayment is manual with no payroll deduction. Risks we show rather than hide: a stronger peso lowers every position's ratio; the oracle rejects prices older than 1 hour, outside 5 to 50 MXN/USD, or jumping more than 20%; the vault, desk and pool are funded separately and capped (10,000 per swap, 1,500 per driver, pool at most 80% lent); OUSD's issuer controls its transfer policy. No peg, yield or credit model is claimed.
