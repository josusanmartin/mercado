07

~12s. Mercado Crédito has three sides. Drivers: Demo Uber Data (synthetic) gives an illustrative score and a credit line of min(15% of median weekly net, 1,500 MXN); the server signs a 15-minute EIP-712 attestation bound to the wallet, pool, chain and nonce, and the pool lends with a 2% fee. Lenders: they fund the pool separately from the vault, can't withdraw lent cash, and earn realized fees only when drivers repay; repayments go back to the pool, not burned. Underwriting API: bots pay per report over HTTP 402 with no API key. On the v2 journey, a driver borrowed 500 xMXN and repaid 510, and realized fees went from 0 to 10. Repayment is manual; nothing is deducted from Uber payouts.
