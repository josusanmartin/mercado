~20s. Lend: uncollateralized credit through the gig economy. Start with the lender: the CreditPool is funded separately from the vault. The driver connects Demo Uber Data, which is synthetic. A simple, reproducible heuristic scores it (steady profile: 96.5) and sets a credit line of min(15% of median weekly net, 1,500 MXN); the actual draw is also capped by pool utilization (80%) and open debt. The server signs an EIP-712 attestation bound to this wallet, pool, chain and nonce that expires in 15 minutes. The pool checks it, burns the nonce and lends with a 2% flat fee; replaying the signature is rejected. The screenshot is the real app's offer screen. The numbers match the live run on Moderato: borrow 500 xMXN, debt 510, and four negative simulations (tampered signature, expired, over the cap, replay) revert with the expected errors. Repayment is manual: the live run repaid 510 and the pool's realized fees went from 0 to 10.
