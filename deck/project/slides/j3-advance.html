03

~25s. Start with the lender: the pool is funded separately from the vault. The driver connects Demo Uber Data (synthetic fixtures, labeled everywhere), and the server explains the credit line from a simple, reproducible heuristic: it's illustrative, not a validated credit model. It signs an EIP-712 attestation that expires quickly and is bound to this borrower, pool, chain and nonce. The pool enforces a 1,500 xMXN per-driver cap and keeps total lending at or under 80% of supplied funds. Repayment is manual; nothing is taken from Uber payouts.
