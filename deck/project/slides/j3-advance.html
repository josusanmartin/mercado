~12s. Mercado Crédito: a driver connects their earnings and draws an advance against them; lenders fund the pool. The product's configured limits: up to MX$1,500 per driver, a 2% flat fee, and the pool lends at most 80% of what lenders supplied. The driver repays as they earn. Q&A context: the score is an illustrative rule on demo earnings data, not a validated credit model; repayment is manual today; Tempo testnet.
