A4

Appendix for Q&A (from review/PRODUCT.md, v0.2). Keep answers conditional where v2 is still a proposal. 01: v1 is live on testnet: issuance, exchange inventory, unsecured lending and xMXN HTTP payment settlement. Tempo gives native token transfers, memos and fee sponsorship; MPP makes an HTTP response payable with a verified receipt. v2 adds the proposed collateral and mint/burn exchange; the design is not yet evidence of a working system. 02: The intended minter is an institution or sophisticated collateral owner; debt is in MXN, valued through an oracle. On Moderato every collateral is a test asset: no fiat redemption, no peg, no real Treasury or equity backing. The driver borrows existing xMXN; they don't open the institutional mint position. 03: Production collateral would carry its own external yield; the vault runs no strategy. Our sOUSD is a demo ERC-4626 with admin-funded accrual, not real income. Review requires donation-resistant accounting before v2 is accepted; a rising share price alone proves neither earned yield nor redeemable assets. 04: No automatic guarantee. For example, OUSD received at 18.37 MXN/USD backs the xMXN it minted only while the rate stays there: if the peso strengthens, the same OUSD covers fewer pesos, and only the equity buffer absorbs the gap. v2 must specify and enforce reserve coverage, a redemption stop or haircut, and recapitalization; until those pass stress tests the vAMM stays in progress. A circuit breaker can stop an unsafe withdrawal; it can't create missing backing. 05: v1 uses three separate pools: CDP collateral, seeded exchange inventory and lender-supplied xMXN. v2 proposes separate accounting for CDP collateral and a protocol OUSD reserve; since xMXN is fungible, redemptions must be bounded by aggregate reserve liabilities. The advance pool stays separately funded and unsecured. 06: In this demo, admin-set prices and income fixtures are explicit trust assumptions. Haircuts and debt ceilings cap exposure but can't make a made-up price or income true. Signed loan limits stop replay and over-drawing, not Sybil fraud or default. 07: It proves verified testnet settlement for the API order; airtime fulfillment is always SIMULATED. Tested single-process recovery and same-order retries avoid a second settlement; that's bounded evidence, not exactly-once. A partner paying for a quote buys the API response, not the swap or a fill after expiry. 08: The demo sponsor has target, function and spender limits, fee caps and process-local quotas; unsigned requests can still use an eligible sender's quota, and restarts reset budgets. It pays fees, not purchases. Before real money: tested v2 solvency and valuation, reliable oracles and liquidity, real asset and fulfillment arrangements, robust recovery, and legal and compliance review.
