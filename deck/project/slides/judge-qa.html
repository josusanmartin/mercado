Appendix for Q&A (from review/PRODUCT.md). 01: Tempo gives native TIP-20 transfers, payment memos, USD-token fees and sponsorship; MPP makes the HTTP request itself payable (unpaid call gets 402, a verified xMXN payment unlocks it). We show a bill endpoint and a bot buying a report with no API key. We have not proved better economics than existing rails. 02: No. Moderato testnet; faucet OUSD with no real value or issuer backing; xMXN is a synthetic peso debt token, minted at 150%+, with no peg, redemption or cash-out. 03: Fewer pesos per dollar lowers collateral value; below 135% liquidation is allowed with a capped bonus. The feed is an admin mock with staleness and sanity guards; real feeds, liquidators and stressed liquidity are production work. 04: No. Vault collateral, desk inventory and pool cash are separately funded, with visible balances. Pool loans are unsecured; lenders can't withdraw lent cash; fees are realized on repayment. 05: We don't solve identity or credit fraud. Income is labeled Demo Uber Data; the attestation binds borrower, chain, pool, limit, expiry and nonce, and the contract enforces caps. That stops signature misuse, not fake income or many wallets. Lenders bear credit risk. 06: Quote and challenge bind to one order; settlement is serialized per order and interrupted attempts are reconciled. Pull-mode crash tests recovered one settlement and one fulfillment. Bounded single-process evidence, not universal exactly-once; duplicate push transfers may need refunds; fulfillment is always SIMULATED. 07: Sponsorship is conditional: Moderato only, approved targets and senders, max 0.01 USD fee each, 30 tx per sender/hour, 500/hour global, reset on restart. A bounded demo sponsor, not production anti-abuse; it pays fees, not purchases. 08: Proof: deployed and source-verified contracts, transfer/borrow/repay/liquidation receipts, real 402 settlement and paid API calls, tests and browser journeys. Missing: approved income access and underwriting, collections, robust oracle and liquidity, production payment recovery and sponsor controls, and commercial and legal setup for real fulfillment or fiat rails.
