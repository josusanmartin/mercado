~10s. Why an ecosystem rather than four apps: each product feeds the others. Mint creates the supply of xMXN. Remesas and Global move it across the border, which creates demand for xMXN and fees for the exchange's reserve buffer. Crédito brings in users, drivers who hold and spend xMXN and lenders who earn realized fees. And Mercado Card plus HTTP 402 payments for bills and paid APIs give xMXN a use in every product (Card is still being built). This is the design thesis; it is not a measured network effect, and we have no usage numbers beyond testnet runs.
