Appendix for Q&A. xMXN, a TIP-20 peso, has two issuers on the live v2 deployment. The MultiCollateralVault issues CDP debt: positions per user and collateral, OUSD and sOUSD at 150% to mint and 135% to liquidate, tTBILL at 200/170, and the mIDX index token at 300/220, each with its own debt ceiling. MoveAMM issues against its own OUSD reserve and tracks that as a liability; at 10:17 UTC its reserve covered about 259% of the 27,948 xMXN it had issued. The credit pool (about 12,040 xMXN of lender capital) and the savings vaults (sOUSD at 5%, sxMXN at 8%, paid from funded reserves) are separate accounts that hold xMXN or OUSD but never mint it. Prices and parameters are owner-controlled on testnet; the FX rate is admin-relayed from Coinbase, not a decentralized oracle.
