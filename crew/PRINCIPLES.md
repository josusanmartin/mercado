# Mercado design guiding principles (josu, 02:58)

Reference: thefutureinblack.com, a "cultural intelligence studio". It's **inspiration for principles, not something to copy**: no use of their name, photos, logo or layouts verbatim. These principles sit above every other design doc. Where DESIGN-ECOSYSTEM §3 or mobile.md conflict, **this file wins**, but keep their structure (IA, product layouts, the mobile app patterns).

## The principles
1. **Editorial confidence.** Type is the hero. Use one massive, ultra-heavy, condensed grotesque for display ("Archivo Black" or "Anton" from Google Fonts, uppercase, tight -0.02em tracking, line-height 0.85–0.9) at sizes that feel almost too big, plus **Inter Tight** for everything else (400/500/600). No third face. Numbers that matter (balances, rates, coverage) are set big and heavy, like headlines.
2. **Black and white first, culture second.** The canvas is white `#FFFFFF` and the ink is pure black `#0A0A0A` (dark mode inverts). Color comes from a **cultural palette** used deliberately, never decoratively: **jade/deep green `#1F3D2B`**, **terracotta `#E2603F`**, **cream `#F2E6C9`**, plus product accents tuned to sit with them (Mint green `#1F3D2B`, Remesas terracotta `#E2603F`, Global cobalt `#2448C8`, Crédito rosa `#D93A7A`, Card ink `#0A0A0A` on cream, Invest maize gold `#E5A823`). Color blocks are large and flat, or they live in imagery.
3. **Image inside type.** The signature move: big words with imagery masked inside (CSS `background-clip: text` with a photo/texture), e.g. "ENVÍA A CASA", "UNA MONEDA". Use it once per page at most, on the hero.
4. **Culture-forward imagery.** Real-feeling Mexican life: markets, family, drivers, corner stores, color and texture. Use only royalty-free photos with a clear license (Unsplash/Pexels license; record source + photographer in `apps/web/public/photos/SOURCES.md`) or our own generated art. No stock clichés (handshakes, laptops), no real brands in photos, no identifiable people in sensitive contexts. Grayscale + one cultural color treatment is welcome.
5. **Small meta, big statement.** Pair huge statements with tiny, precise meta text (11–12px, uppercase or small caps): "CDMX · 18.37 MXN/USD · 02:58", "■ 02 · REMESAS", thin 1px rules, index numbers. That's where honesty labels live too: small, precise, always present ("TESTNET · SIN VALOR REAL").
6. **Layered, asymmetric layout.** Let an image overlap a headline, offset columns, and leave generous white space. One idea per screen region. No grids of equal boxes, no card soup.
7. **Restraint in UI chrome.** Black pill buttons (`menu`-style), hairline dividers, almost no shadows, no gradients, no rounded-everything. Corners are 999px for pills, 0–8px elsewhere.
8. **Motion with intent.** Scroll-driven horizontal type pans, slow reveals, number tick-ups. Never bouncy or gimmicky.

## How it applies
- **Web app (desktop):** Home hero = a giant "UNA MONEDA. SEIS PRODUCTOS." with image-in-type, a live meta line (CDMX · rate · time · TESTNET), then the six products as an editorial index (large numbered rows "01 MINT — Las instituciones crean xMXN" with the live number at right), not tiles. Product pages: a huge product name, a tiny meta line, big unboxed KPIs, hairline sections. Keep keyboard nav and every label.
- **Phone apps:** keep the fintech structure from mobile.md v2 (one big balance, round actions, bank-style lists), but restyle to these principles: a white (or ink, for Card) canvas, Archivo Black/Anton for the balance and screen titles, Inter Tight elsewhere, the cultural accent per product, tiny uppercase meta labels, and an optional image-in-type header ("ENVÍA") on Remesas home. Spanish-only in-app.
- **Deck:** already editorial and dark. Align it: Archivo Black/Anton for statements, the cultural palette, at least one image-in-type statement slide, and small meta labels on every slide.

Owners: `web` (shared tokens, components/console, Home, shell) FIRST, then `mobile` (kit + Remesas + Card), `mobile2` (Crédito + Invest), `api`/`contracts` (their pages via shared components), `slides` (the deck). `review` checks labels/contrast (terracotta on white for small text fails AA, so use it for large text and blocks only).

## Header rule (josu, 03:02)
**No KPI strip under product headers, on any page.** The three-number band ('USD/MXN 18.37 · Exchange backing 270% · Familia balance 4,066', 'Available to lend · Advances out · Fees paid') is useless there. A number only appears where the user needs it to act (e.g. the rate inside the send quote, 'available' inside the draw sheet, coverage inside the Risk tab).
